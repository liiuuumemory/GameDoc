# Naming System（命名系统）

This document defines the naming rules for The Line（线）. It should be used when adding new episode scripts, worldbuilding notes, character files, location notes, system notes, or development records.

本文档用于统一 The Line（线）的项目标题、地名、机构名、数据术语、居住状态、角色名和章节名。新增文档时应优先遵循本规范。

## Project Title（项目标题）

Use:

- The Line（线）
- The Line: Northbank（线：北岸）

Do not use more explanatory translations such as “适居线” or “第七线”. The Chinese title should remain simply 线.

## Places and Districts（地名与区段）

Use:

- The Line（线）：the wider boundary and administrative condition of habitation.
- Section 7（第七区段）：the administrative section where the first story takes place.
- Northbank（北岸）：the primary district for the first story.
- Survey Office（测绘办公室）：the survey office or field workplace.
- Market Street（市场街）：the lived-in street where residents still gather.
- Old Blocks（旧街区）：the blocks outside current residential recognition.
- Pump House 7（七号泵房）：the pump house and facility point.
- Gate Two（二号门）：the maintenance entrance for facility access.
- Shade System（遮光系统）：the broader shading infrastructure.

Names should feel like real city, administrative, or infrastructure names. Avoid names that sound like quest areas, dungeon labels, or ornate setting terms.

## Institutions（机构）

Use:

- The Authority（管理局）
- Survey Office（测绘办公室）

The Authority（管理局） handles boundaries, Residential Status（居住状态）, permits, and reviews. Survey Office（测绘办公室） is the practical work unit and field-facing location.

Avoid grand institutional names involving “晨昏”, “星球”, “中央”, or similar setting-heavy language.

## Layers and Data（图层与数据）

Use:

- Orbital Layer（轨道图层）
- Ground Layer（地面图层）
- Model Discrepancy（模型偏差）
- Discrepancy Note（偏差附录）
- Residential Threshold（居住阈值）
- Field Review（现场复核）
- Temporary Review（临时复核）

For Chapter 1: Outside（第一章：线外）, the key technical sentence is:

> Field readings from the Old Blocks do not match the Orbital Layer within accepted tolerance.

Chinese wording:

> Old Blocks（旧街区）的现场读数与 Orbital Layer（轨道图层）之间的偏差超出允许范围。

Do not reveal later causes in early documents. Avoid terms such as Deep Heat Ridge, Black Sun, Underground Heat Source, or any equivalent Chinese phrasing outside specific ending documents.

## Residential Status（居住状态）

Use:

- Residential Status（居住状态）
- Conditional Residence（条件居住）
- Buffer Status（缓冲状态）
- Restricted Residence（限制居住）
- Review Pending（待复核）

Report actions should use:

- Maintain Buffer Status（维持缓冲状态）
- Delay Status Change（暂缓状态变更）
- Report Unregistered Residence（上报未登记居住）
- File a Discrepancy Note（提交偏差附录）
- File Without Names（不记名提交）

Do not use color-grade language such as red, yellow, or gray zones.

## Characters（角色）

Use:

- the surveyor（测绘员）
- Mara Chen（陈玛拉）
- Jun（俊）
- Mr. Park（朴先生）
- Ms. Ward（沃德女士）

The protagonist should usually remain the surveyor（测绘员） in planning documents. Avoid giving the protagonist a strongly branded name. In dialogue, residents may naturally call Jun by his English name.

## Chapter Titles（章节标题）

Use:

- Chapter 1: Outside（第一章：线外）
- Chapter 2: Review（第二章：复核）
- Chapter 3: Failure（第三章：故障）
- Chapter 4: Decision（第四章：决定）
- True Ending: A Place Still Warm（真结局：仍有余温之地）

## True Ending Title（真结局标题）

**A Place Still Warm（仍有余温之地）**

This title should be reserved for the true ending. It should not be used as a chapter title, ordinary quest title, area name, or system label. It refers to the places that remain warm outside official maps, outside accepted models, and outside the language of administrative recognition.

该标题只保留给真结局，不作为普通章节名、任务名、区域名或系统名使用。它指向那些仍在官方地图之外、模型之外、行政语言之外保持余温的地方。
